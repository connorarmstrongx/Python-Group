03 Process

A considered process, from first conversation to handover.

Building a high-end home is a long relationship. Our consulting process keeps decisions clear, costs understood and quality protected at every stage.

  1. 01

    Conversation

    We begin by listening: your brief, your site, your timeline and how you want to live in the home. We tell you plainly what is realistic.

  2. 02

    Planning and design coordination

    We work alongside your architect or designer, or help you find one, testing the design against buildability, budget and programme before it is locked in.

  3. 03

    Scope, budget and build plan

    We define scope and allowances, and our Head of Construction, a licensed Master Builder, plans and prices the build. Pricing is explained clearly and reviewed with you.

  4. 04

    Consents and preparation

    Consents, engineering, specification and programme are coordinated so that construction begins with the important decisions already made.

  5. 05

    Construction oversight

    Our Head of Construction leads the build on your behalf: programme, quality, variations and communication, with regular updates and inspection of key stages.

  6. 06

    Handover

    A careful final inspection, completion documentation and a clear handover, so you move into the home with confidence.

Our builder

Our builder is our own Head of Construction, a licensed Master Builder, not a third party. Restricted building work is carried out or supervised by Licensed Building Practitioners, as the Building Act requires.

06 About

Quality over volume. Detail over display.

Python Group is a New Zealand business specialising in high-end residential construction. We guide private clients through the planning, design coordination and building of exceptional homes, and we undertake selected developments of our own.

Our role is to bring clarity to a complex process. Architects, designers and our own Head of Construction, a licensed Master Builder, work around a single standard, and we protect that standard from the first sketch to the final handover. We are deliberately selective, so that every home receives real attention, and we communicate plainly at every stage.
